About the Challenge

The Human Mobility Challenge 2026 invites researchers to develop models that predict, simulate, and explain human mobility patterns. The challenge aims to create a reproducible benchmark for urban AI, spatial data science, and human mobility modeling.

Challenge Tasks

Task 1: Next-location Prediction

Predict the next visited location given a sequence of previous visits.

Task 2: Trajectory Generation

Generate realistic mobility trajectories that reproduce observed patterns.

Task 3: Cross-city Transfer

Evaluate whether mobility models generalize across urban contexts.

Task 4: Counterfactual Prediction

Predict mobility responses under hypothetical urban interventions.

Dataset access instructions will be announced soon. The challenge will provide training, validation, and test data for evaluating human mobility prediction and simulation models.
